A newspaper front page summing up Thursday 19 November 2026, drawn for legibility at thumbnail size: Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen for the first three-way summit of the US, China and Russia leaders; Grand Theft Auto VI launches worldwide after 13 years; Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth; and the world court rules on the Rohingya genocide case — plus the rest of the day's news in a three-line bottom ticker. The program draws the whole page itself in Node with only the standard library: it decodes four fresh panels made for this day's stories, lays them out in a lead-story front page under a masthead, and sets every headline, region kicker and caption with a small bitmap face drawn in the code, now with bold strokes and larger sub-headlines so the page stays legible at thumbnail size.
